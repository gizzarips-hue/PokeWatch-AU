PokeWatch AU Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

At PokeWatch AU, we commit to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines what limited data we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that data.

1. Information We Collect

To function effectively within your Discord server, the Bot only processes information standard to Discord operations: Server IDs (to link configurations), Channel IDs (to route alerts), and User IDs (to check command permissions).

2. How We Use Information

The minimal data we process is used exclusively to deliver automated mapping, tracking, and notification services requested by your server. We do not sell or trade your data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Server configurations are stored only for as long as the Bot remains in your server. If the Bot is kicked or removed, your configuration data is marked for deletion.

4. Third-Party Services

Our services rely completely on the Discord API. Your use of this Bot is also governed by Discord’s Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights and Data Erasure

Server administrators can clear custom configurations using the Bot's configuration commands. For direct database removal requests, please reach out to us via our support channels.
